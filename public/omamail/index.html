关于 Omamail / About

Omamail 是一款键盘优先的桌面邮件与日历客户端，供本站作者个人访问自己的邮箱账户（Gmail、Outlook、IMAP/JMAP 等）使用。它运行在作者自己的电脑上，仅在本地处理邮件数据。

Omamail is a keyboard-first desktop email and calendar client, used by the owner of this site to access the owner's own mailboxes. It runs entirely on the owner's own computer and processes mail data locally only.
